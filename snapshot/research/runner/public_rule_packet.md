Using the public problem in Daria Poliakova, More (shifted) runners, less loneliness, https://arxiv.org/html/2609.23952v1, derive a new runner-saving local uniform replacement rule or a structural construction that generalizes her rules. This request contains only the public problem and definitions. Start from even velocities 2,4,...,2N at zero phase, covering at radius delta=1/(N+1). For each originally responsible speed q and primitive numerator k, a replacement speed v at phase a+h delta gives coverage of z in [-1,1] when vk/q+a is integral and |(v/q)z+h|<=1. We seek particularly a verified uniform saving of one with largest affected velocity below 192 (her Theorem 7 uses 192), or two with largest velocity below 240. If an explicit rule is unavailable, look for a proved reduction of the uniform geometric coverage problem to a smaller combinatorial or arithmetic problem with a materially broader search than the published 2,3,5-smooth recipes. A rigorous endpoint reduction for continuous phases/slope parameters would also be helpful. Avoid hypotheses presented as proofs. Do not claim results of unexecuted code.
