The partition result gives simple negative roots and pairwise strict interlacing in every five-row window for sufficiently large starting rows. The threshold is existential. Rows 4 to 2004 have a separate exact verification. The phase paper gives the exact local formula on a sharp centred cube for consecutive speeds at prime modulus and a wider arithmetic obstruction.
