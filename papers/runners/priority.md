# Prior work and scope

Checked 27 November 2026. Poliakova's arXiv record currently lists v1. Her Theorem 1 gives $E_n\ge\lfloor n/287\rfloor$, Theorem 2 gives $E_n\ge1$ for $n\ge95$, and Definition 5 and Theorems 6–7 supply the local uniform rule framework and its disjoint dilation. Theorem 7 and the $19\to17$, $24\to21$ rows of Table 1 supply the three literal base rules here. The largest coefficient listed in Table 1 is $1/224$. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23952) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.23952v1)

The first MathLab paper combined mutually compatible prescriptions, including some shared speeds, and proved $\lfloor n/150\rfloor$ and $\liminf E_n/n\ge513079/69470921$. The stronger bound uses selected prescriptions where source tables disagree, checks every affected even slot again, and uses the new sixteen-entry schedule. The paper retains the v1 finite decision theorem for the affine witness class. The public searches by aadrianvv already apply SAT to finite affine phase menus and to fixed-size patches on the even seed. Those are predecessors for the local witness computation. [MathLab v1](https://github.com/F1Justin/MathLab/blob/ef98c0707e3a9e1b6b66f905aadd24ea6e38cadc/papers/runners/compatible-replacements.pdf) · [Uniform rule search](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/110#issuecomment-7055867934) · [Fixed-size patch search](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/110#issuecomment-7060116112)

The finite counterexamples are a separate line. Blanco, Criado and Santos establish $5\le n\le17$ and reproduce Poliakova's asymptotic bound. Xavier Callens's preprint, indexed by aiXiv as 2611.00005 and dated 31 October 2026, reports independently produced exact certificates for $18\le n\le38$. The solved-conjectures package has certificates for $18\le n\le57$, due to aadrianvv and tomdif, and Davis's Lean verification of their exact maxima. The quantitative schedule applies above this interval and leaves Zhang's remaining $58\le n\le94$ sizes open.
